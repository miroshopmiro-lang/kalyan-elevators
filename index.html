Welcome to Kalyan Elevators

Kalyan Elevators is a Kerala-based elevator company specializing in the design, manufacturing, installation and maintenance of modern lift solutions. We make luxury home lifts for villas and homes, and provide passenger lifts and commercial lifts for apartments, offices, hospitals and shops across Kerala. Our home lifts install easily in existing homes as well as houses under construction.
