About Kalyan Elevators

Established in Thrissur in 2018. Elevators for homes, residential and commercial buildings.

Who we are

Kalyan Elevators Pvt. Ltd. was established in Thrissur in 2018, beginning with lift annual maintenance contracts (AMC) and modernisation. Today we are one of Kerala's top-rated premium luxury home lift companies. We manufacture, install, modernise and maintain elevators, with offices in Thrissur and Kochi.

We provide conventional machine-room and machine-room-less (MRL) elevator packages for homes, apartments, offices, hotels, hospitals and commercial buildings. Our services also cover escalators for malls and commercial complexes.

Our luxury home lifts can be installed in existing homes and houses under construction, with customised cabins and a choice of Golden Mirror, Rose Gold Mirror, Black Mirror and Full Glass finishes.
