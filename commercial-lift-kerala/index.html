About commercial lifts

Passenger lifts for shops, showrooms, offices, hotels and malls, with cabin finishes and doors selected for the building.

We also provide goods and freight lifts, car lifts for multi-storey parking, and escalator services for malls and commercial complexes. Each application has its own load, door and space requirements.

Suitable for

  • Shops
  • Showrooms
  • Offices
  • Hotels
  • Malls

Planning details

Goods / freight range100 to 7000 kg, according to the application
Car lift example3000 kg; 0.1 to 0.3 m/s
Door optionsCollapsible, automatic or double-swing, according to the lift
Other servicesEscalators for malls and commercial complexes

Goods and car figures describe separate brochure configurations. Passenger lifts are selected for people; goods handling and parking requirements need their own specification.

Frequently asked questions

What does a commercial lift cost in Kerala?

It depends on the building, the number of floors, the lift space and the cabin you choose. Send us those details on WhatsApp or call us, and we will come back with a price for your project.

Do you install and maintain commercial lifts?

Yes. From site inspection and lift selection to installation, commissioning and maintenance, our team supports customers throughout the elevator lifecycle.

Which parts of Kerala do you cover?

We work across Kerala from our offices in Angamaly, Thrissur and Kochi, including Kozhikode, Palakkad, Kottayam and Malappuram.
