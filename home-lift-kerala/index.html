Frequently asked questions

How much does a home lift cost in Kerala?

Our luxury home lift designs are listed from ₹20 lakh to ₹30 lakh. The price for your home depends on the design, the finish, the number of floors and the space available, so send us those details for a quote.

How much space does a home lift need?

Most of our designs need a 1.2 m by 1.2 m space. The curved glass design needs 1.80 m by 1.50 m. The lift needs less pit space and less overhead, and mounts on one side wall.

Can you install a lift in a house that is already built?

Yes. Our home lifts install easily in pre-existing homes as well as houses under construction.

What finishes can I choose?

Golden Mirror, Rose Gold Mirror, Black Mirror and Full Glass. Cabins and colour codes are customised for each home.

How many people can a home lift carry?

Our 1.2 m designs carry 200 kg, which is two people and a child. The curved glass design carries 272 kg, four people.

Does a home lift need three phase power?

No. Single phase and three phase are both available.

Can a home lift take a wheelchair?

Yes, a wheelchair lift is possible. Tell us when you enquire.

Do you service the lifts you install?

Yes. From site inspection and lift selection to installation, commissioning and maintenance, our team supports customers throughout the elevator lifecycle. 24 hours service available.
