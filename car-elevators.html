Overview

The garage,
reimagined.

Whether lifting a grand tourer into a penthouse gallery or cycling inventory through a multi-level showroom, our car elevators move vehicles with millimetre-level precision. Reinforced platforms carry up to 5,000 kg, and structural safety floor locks secure the deck rigidly at every landing.

Dual control panels — positioned for operation directly from the driver's window — mean no one leaves the car until it has arrived.
