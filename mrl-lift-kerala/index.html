About MRL lifts

MRL means machine room-less. A compact gearless motor sits inside the lift shaft, so the building needs no separate machine room on the roof.

It is a common choice for new buildings, and can be planned to open up to the terrace floor.

Suitable for

  • Apartments
  • Villas
  • Offices
  • Hospitals
  • Buildings with limited roof space

How an MRL lift works

  1. Gearless motor in the shaft

    A compact gearless motor turns the traction sheave directly, with no gearbox.

  2. Ropes and counterweight

    Ropes or belts move the cabin and a counterweight in opposite directions.

  3. Electronic drive

    The drive controls acceleration, speed and levelling for a smooth stop at each floor.

Benefits

  • No machine room on the roof
  • Civil construction cost saving
  • Opening up to the terrace floor
  • High levelling accuracy
  • Smooth and comfortable ride
  • Low vibration and noise level
  • No gearbox, so no gearbox oil and no oil leakage
  • Less maintenance and a longer life time

What the building needs

Machine roomNot required
ShaftRoom for the cabin, counterweight and machine
Pit and overheadStill required. Depth and height depend on the model
PowerSingle or three phase, by model

Good to know

  • MRL removes the machine room, not the pit or the overhead.
  • Service access is inside the shaft, so shaft sizes must be accurate.
  • Energy and maintenance savings depend on the model and how much the lift is used.

Exact pit, overhead, shaft and power for your building are confirmed at the site inspection.

Machine room, MRL or hydraulic?

Machine room and MRL side by side

  1. Machine room lift

    Motor and controller in a separate room on the roof.

  2. MRL lift

    A compact gearless motor inside the shaft. No roof machine room.

  3. Same ride, less building

    Both are traction lifts. MRL saves the machine room and its civil cost.

Frequently asked questions

What is an MRL lift?

MRL stands for machine room-less. A compact gearless motor sits inside the lift shaft, so no separate roof machine room is needed.

Does an MRL lift need a pit and overhead space?

Yes. MRL means no separate machine room. The pit and overhead clearance are still needed, and depend on the model.

Does a gearless MRL lift need oil changes?

It has no gearbox, so there is no gearbox oil to change. Other parts are still serviced on a regular schedule.

What does a MRL lift cost in Kerala?

It depends on the building, the number of floors, the lift space and the cabin you choose. Send us those details on WhatsApp or call us, and we will come back with a price for your project.

Yes. From site inspection and lift selection to installation, commissioning and maintenance, our team supports customers throughout the elevator lifecycle.
