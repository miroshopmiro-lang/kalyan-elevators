About machine room lifts

A machine room lift is an electric traction lift. The motor, gearbox and controller sit in a separate room above the lift shaft.

It is the established system for apartments, offices and commercial buildings, and the right choice where a building already has a machine room.

Suitable for

  • Apartments
  • Offices
  • Hotels
  • Commercial buildings
  • Buildings with an existing machine room

How a machine room lift works

  1. Motor and gearbox

    An electric motor turns the traction sheave through a reduction gearbox.

  2. Ropes and counterweight

    Steel ropes over the sheave move the cabin and a counterweight in opposite directions.

  3. Controller and brake

    The controller and electromagnetic brake manage speed and stop the cabin level at each floor.

Benefits

  • Speed from 0.3 m/s to 2 m/s
  • Compact mono block machine
  • High torque and high static load capacity
  • Efficient electromagnetic brake system
  • Low vibration and noise level
  • Low power loss and low power consumption
  • A separate room for servicing the machinery

What the building needs

Machine roomRequired, above the shaft
ShaftEnclosed lift well, 230 mm brick or 150 mm RCC
Pit and overheadRequired. Depth and height depend on speed and model
PowerThree phase for most buildings, confirmed per project

Good to know

  • The machine room takes extra space on the roof.
  • The gearbox needs oil and periodic servicing.
  • An existing machine room lift can sometimes be modernised. We assess the shaft first.

Exact pit, overhead, shaft and power for your building are confirmed at the site inspection.

Machine room, MRL or hydraulic?

Machine room and MRL side by side

  1. Machine room lift

    Motor and controller in a separate room on the roof.

  2. MRL lift

    A compact gearless motor inside the shaft. No roof machine room.

  3. Same ride, less building

    Both are traction lifts. MRL saves the machine room and its civil cost.

Frequently asked questions

Does a machine room lift need a separate room?

Yes. The lifting machine and controller sit in a dedicated room, usually above the lift shaft.

Are geared lifts outdated or unsafe?

No. Geared traction is an established technology. Safety depends on the equipment, the installation, inspection and maintenance.

Can a machine room lift be changed to an MRL lift?

Sometimes. The shaft size, structure and electrical supply have to be checked first. We assess it at a site inspection.

What does a machine room lift cost in Kerala?

It depends on the building, the number of floors, the lift space and the cabin you choose. Send us those details on WhatsApp or call us, and we will come back with a price for your project.

Yes. From site inspection and lift selection to installation, commissioning and maintenance, our team supports customers throughout the elevator lifecycle.
