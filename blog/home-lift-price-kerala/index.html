Home lift price in Kerala

In shortListed prices: ₹20 lakh to ₹30 lakh. Five designs need a 1.2 m x 1.2 m space and carry 200 kg. The curved glass design needs 1.80 m x 1.50 m and carries 272 kg. The final price depends on your site.

Listed prices

  • Luxury Golden Lift Designs: ₹20,00,000. Golden mirror finish doors, 1.2 m x 1.2 m room, 200 kg.
  • Luxury Designs (golden mirror etched design): ₹25,00,000. 1.2 m x 1.2 m room, 200 kg.
  • Luxury Curved Glass Lift Design: ₹28,00,000. 1.80 m x 1.50 m room, 272 kg (4 people).
  • Premium Luxury Design: ₹30,00,000. 1.2 m x 1.2 m room, 200 kg.
  • Luxury Lift For Luxury Homes: ₹30,00,000. 1.2 m x 1.2 m room, 200 kg.
  • Luxury Designs (glass lift): ₹30,00,000. 1.2 m x 1.2 m room, 200 kg.

200 kg = 2 persons and 1 child. 272 kg = 4 persons.

Difference between the designs

All six are home glass lifts with a steel structure lift room and glass cladding. The difference is in the cabin and finish, the cabin size and the drive. Five are hydraulic. The curved glass design is hydraulic or traction.

Final price

The final price depends on the available space, number of floors, capacity and the design of the house. We confirm it after a site visit.

Included in every design

  • Steel structure lift room with glass cladding
  • Single phase and three phase power available
  • Customised cabin and colour codes
  • Less pit space and less overhead needed, mounted on one side wall
  • Non-lubricant guides and rails, and emergency landing switches
  • Installs in pre-existing homes; a wheelchair lift is possible
  • 24 hours service available

Confirming your specification

Our home lift range includes a 230V AC single-phase configuration with a 300 mm pit, for indoor or outdoor installation in a masonry shaft or self-supporting structure. Power, pit and overhead requirements are confirmed for the selected lift and site.

Our brochure lists Automatic Rescue Device (ARD), floor announcements and fireman operation among the control options. The controls and safety equipment included are confirmed for the selected lift in your quotation.
